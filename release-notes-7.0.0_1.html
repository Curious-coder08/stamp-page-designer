Release 7.0.0 — (date of release)

In short: a Text tile for headings and notes, captions over several lines, text inside the stamp frame, solid or dashed frame lines, finer positioning for pocket sheets, a smarter Auto-align, country flags, your own pictures, a Title tab and Margin text, album files you can download and open again, frames round your stamps, new border art, and Save as PDF made right in the page. Albums from 6.0.0 open exactly as you left them.

  • Text tile (new): plain text with no border, up to 4 lines, placed anywhere on the page. Left, centre or right alignment; Tight, Normal or Relaxed line spacing; all 18 fonts with size, bold, italic, underline and a text colour; plus "First line bold" and "First line underline" for a heading over plain lines. Width set with the blue corners; the height follows the text. Typing a 5th line is allowed but the box turns red, "Before printing" mentions it, and only the first 4 lines print.
  • Captions over several lines: a "Multiple lines" switch on each stamp allows up to 4 lines, each as wide as the stamp; the caption strip grows to 10, 15 or 20 mm and spacing is still measured below it. One line remains the default.
  • Text inside the stamp frame: an optional short text (up to 10 characters, e.g. a denomination) centred in the box, in the caption font at up to 8 pt. It prints by default; when switched off it shows on screen only, marked with a small crossed-out printer icon.
  • Stamp frame line: Dashed grey (as before) or Solid black, set in Page settings for the whole page, with a per-stamp override. Triangles follow the page choice; saved settings and "Use this setup on all pages" include it. Sizes and spacing are unchanged.
  • S-numbers now sit in the upper-left corner of each stamp box (screen and Preview only; never printed).
  • Page Header, Subheader and Divider position: Far left, Left, Centre, Right or Far right once the box is narrower than full width. The text inside follows, and Auto-align keeps the chosen place.
  • Page numbers can have "Page" before the number.
  • Fine positioning for pocket sheets: Alt + Shift + arrow (Option + Shift on a Mac) moves the selection 0.625 mm, with faint 0.625 mm lines at 200% zoom (screen only). Auto-align leaves fine-moved items where you put them. A folded "Exact position" section shows an item's left, top and bottom distance from the corner of the paper.
  • Keyboard shortcuts card: a "Shortcuts" button next to Help, or press ?.
  • Make these the same size: select several stamps, type one width and height, and they all change in one Undo step.
  • Auto-align: a divider now keeps its place between its sections, with row spacing above and below; rows are laid out around pictures and Text boxes, never on top of them; and on a page with pictures or Text boxes Auto-align first explains that these do not move, with Align around them or Cancel.
  • Country flags: 255 flags (every country, plus England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland, the EU and the UN) in the Art panel, with a search box — type "Bel" for Belgium. Flags print in their own colours. From the MIT-licensed flag-icons collection.
  • Your own pictures: put a scan, photo or logo from your computer in a picture box — JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF up to 25 MB. Big pictures are made smaller for you (about 1500 pixels across); see-through parts stay see-through; they print in their own colours. iPhone HEIC photos get a message asking to save them as JPEG first. Each picture is kept once, apart from the album, so using it on many pages costs nothing extra.
  • Picture colour: line pictures can follow the page border (as before), take one of the border colours, or any colour from the colour picker.
  • Title tab (new): a small label on the top-left or top-right corner of the page border, partly outside it, with 1–2 lines, an optional line round it and an optional flag. The border breaks behind it and that corner's ornament is left out.
  • Margin text (new): one line reading bottom to top in the left or right margin, slid up and down, with optional flags. It keeps clear of the punch holes and the printer's 4 mm edge, and says so when a margin is too narrow.
  • Download album and Open album file: save a whole album, pictures included, as one .stampalbum file, and open it again into one of the 3 slots (asking before replacing one). A good habit on Safari, which may clear a site's storage after about a week without a visit.
  • Frames (new): an ornamental rectangle round a stamp, a set or a row — one, two or three lines, a stamp-edge perforation (with or without a thin inner rule), or Hidden for grouping only (never printed). Thin, Medium or Thick; square or rounded corners; any colour. Select stamps and press Frame to fit one round them, 2.5 or 5 mm clear; the stamps keep their own borders. Everything inside moves with the frame.
  • Auto-align and frames: on the page a frame and its contents count as one item; with a frame selected, Auto-align arranges only what is inside it and grows the frame if needed — never shrinking a stamp, and telling you if the frame can't grow.
  • New border art: the Ashoka Chakra (24-spoke wheel) as a pattern, a corner and a divider ornament — it turns Navy when chosen; lotus and rose patterns and corners; and seated lion, beaver, kangaroo and eagle corners. All original drawings, centred on the corner and upright at the bottom too.
  • Save as PDF made right in the page (website): no print window — one click saves a true-size PDF to your Downloads, with sharp, searchable text. One album can mix paper sizes (Letter, A4…), each page at its own size. Then print the PDF at 100% / Actual size. If a browser can't make it, the old print-window route is offered.
  • Classic stamps in Clip Art: 20 engraved-style drawings of famous early stamps (Basel Dove style, Bullseye style, Three-penny Beaver style, Bluenose schooner style and more), each added 40 mm on its long side.
  • The Divider button now opens a chooser of styles first, like Clip Art — nothing is added until you pick.
  • Title tab size: make the tab wider or taller (never smaller than its text); Fit to text puts it back.
  • Margin text is not used with Dense margins (too crowded): the button is greyed out, and on a page switched to Dense it is kept but hidden.
  • Any colour for the page border and its corners: a Custom colour next to the 8 border colours.
  • "Art" is now "Clip Art", with flags and your own pictures too. Pressing it no longer drops a picture on the page: the panel shows every picture, Choose a flag… and Your own picture…, and nothing is added until you pick one (or Cancel).
  • Title tab and Margin text can have a flag before the text, after it, or both — the same flag twice or two different ones, for joint issues.
  • Up to two Margin texts in each side margin (Left 1, Left 2, Right 1, Right 2), each sliding on its own.
  • The add buttons above the page now share one row and wrap on smaller screens instead of scrolling out of sight.
  • Fixed during testing: captions over several lines keep their line breaks; perforated frames keep their clear gap inside the drawn edge; flags in Margin text stay upright.
  • Saved albums, saved settings and Continue work from 6.0.0 and 5.1.0 open and print unchanged.
