Stamp Page Designer

Make true-size stamp album pages, quickly, in your browser — no software to install.

This is a hobby tool for stamp collectors. You build a page by dragging stamp boxes to their real size in millimetres, on a 2.5 mm grid, with the spacing and mount clearance you choose. Then you preview it and print it, or save it as a PDF, at 100% — so what you see on screen is what prints, stamp for stamp.

What it does, in short:

  • Stamp boxes at exact sizes — Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large, or any custom rectangle, up to a 210 × 210 mm plate block on A3.
  • Page Headers and Subheaders, description boxes, and a caption strip under every stamp.
  • S-numbers that follow each stamp in page order, even when you move, copy, or undo it.
  • Paper sizes Letter, A4, Legal, and A3, plus 12 album formats (Scott, Minkus, Davo, H. E. Harris, Stanley Gibbons, Palo, Lighthouse, White Ace, Mystic Heirloom, Schaubek), portrait only, with Classic or Dense margins and an optional punch-hole margin.
  • Warnings when stamps overlap or sit closer than your chosen spacing, counted by item, not by pair.
  • Preview that hides the working grid and colours, and Print / Save as PDF at 100% only — never “Fit to page”.
  • Corner pictures to personalise a page (23 pictures, in the page-border colour), row dividers, Tight 5 mm spacing, snap to neighbours while dragging, and 18 fonts.

What this tool is — and what it is not

It is a visual page designer that runs in your browser. You drag stamp boxes onto the page, they snap to the grid and to each other, and Auto-align can line a whole page up for you. You can set a box to an exact size in millimetres if you want to, but you do not have to type coordinates for every box. What you see on screen, at true size, is what prints.

It is not:

  • Not a form-only or coordinate-only tool. Dragging, snapping and Auto-align are how most pages are actually made.
  • Not a download to install, and not a Java or XML generator. There is nothing to install and no XML to write — it opens in a modern browser on PC or Mac.
  • Not cloud-based. There is no account and no cloud copy. Your work lives in this browser, on this device, as explained below.
  • Not a tool for copying an existing printed page pocket for pocket. It is for designing your own page from scratch, in your own layout.
  • Not a picture tool. There are no stamp pictures inside the boxes, by design. The boxes are for your real stamps. That keeps the pages light and the tool fast.
  • Not a one-command page maker. It does not generate a finished page from a single instruction — you place the boxes, then Auto-align lines them up.

Please read this before you start: Your albums and settings live in this browser, on this device. There is no account, no cloud copy, and no project file to download or open. If you clear this browser's storage, your saved albums go with it. Your printed PDF is your permanent record — save that, and you can rebuild a page from it in under a minute.

Save project and Print / Save as PDF are two different things. Save project keeps your editable page — paper, stamps, positions, captions, headers — in the browser, so you can come back and change it. Print / Save as PDF is the finished output only, and a PDF cannot be reopened as a project.

A hobby project, made for collectors, by a collector. Free to use, and shared in the hope it saves someone an evening of cutting and pasting.
