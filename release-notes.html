Release Notes

A running list, newest first. Entries are added, never removed.

Release 7.0.0 — 5 November 2026

In short: a Text tile for headings and notes, captions over several lines, text inside the stamp frame, and Save as PDF made right in the page.

  • Text tile (new): plain text with no border, up to 4 lines, placed anywhere on the page. Left, centre or right aligned, in the font and size you choose.
  • Captions over several lines: a "Multiple lines" switch on each stamp allows up to 4 lines, each up to 24 characters, with Left, Centre or Right alignment.
  • Text inside the stamp frame: an optional short text (up to 10 characters, e.g. a denomination) centred in the middle of the frame; the text size follows the stamp.
  • Stamp frame line: Dashed grey (as before) or Solid black, set in Page settings for the whole page.
  • S-numbers now sit in the upper-left corner of each stamp box (screen and Preview only; never printed, and they do not move the stamps).
  • Page Header, Subheader and Divider position: Far left, Left, Centre, Right or Far right once the header is left- or right-cut and the page width is set.
  • Page numbers can have "Page" before the number.
  • Fine positioning for pocket sheets: Alt + Shift + arrow (Option + Shift on a Mac) moves the selected item 0.5 mm up, down, left or right.
  • Keyboard shortcuts card: a "Shortcuts" button next to Help, or press ?.
  • Make these the same size: select several stamps, type one width and height, and they all change to it (width and height set separately).
  • Auto-align: a divider now keeps its place between its sections, with row spacing above and below it, so sections keep their order.
  • Country flags: 255 flags (every country, plus England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland, the EU, and the Confederate States, 1861–1865).
  • Your own pictures: put a scan, photo or logo from your computer in a picture box — JPEG, PNG, WebP or a flat SVG — sized to fit the frame, or stretch to fill it.
  • Picture colour: line pictures can follow the page border (as before), take one of the border colours, or be printed as is (for your own colour scans, photos or logos).
  • Title tab (new): a small label on the top-left or top-right corner of the page border, partly outside it, with up to two lines and its own colour.
  • Margin text (new): one line reading bottom to top in the left or right margin, slid up and down, in the border colour, or with a two-tone flag effect.
  • Download album and Open album file: save a whole album, pictures included, as one .stampalbum file, and open it again later; the old browser-saved files are still there.
  • Frames (new): an ornamental rectangle round a stamp, a set or a row — one, two or three lines, in the colour you choose, with gaps for labels and a corner picture.
  • Auto-align and frames: on the page a frame and its contents count as one item; with a frame selected the keep/space panel shows the frame's spacing.
  • New border art: the Ashoka Chakra (24-spoke wheel) as a pattern, a corner and a divider ornament, in the colour of the border, with 26 patterns and 19 corners now.
  • Save as PDF made right in the page (website): no print window — one click saves a true-size PDF of this page, or a whole album, with its own name (album, page, date).
  • Classic stamps in Clip Art: 20 engraved-style drawings of famous early stamps (Basel Dove style, black on white, country's name added).
  • The Divider button now opens a chooser of styles first, like Clip Art — nothing is added until you pick one, and you can close the chooser without adding.
  • Title tab size: make the tab wider or taller (never smaller than its text); Fit to text puts it back to the size of its text.
  • Margin text is not used with Dense margins (too crowded): the button is greyed out, and on a page with Dense margins it does nothing at all.
  • Any colour for the page border and its corners: a Custom colour next to the 8 border colours.
  • "Art" is now "Clip Art", with flags and your own pictures too. Pressing it no longer drops a picture — it opens the chooser, and you can close it without adding.
  • Title tab and Margin text can have a flag before the text, after it, or both — the same flag twice counts for the character limit once.
  • Up to two Margin texts in each side margin (Left 1, Left 2, Right 1, Right 2), each sliding on its own; they never overlap — they join instead.
  • The add buttons above the page now share one row and wrap on smaller screens instead of scrolling sideways.
  • Fixed during testing: captions over several lines keep their line breaks; perforated frames keep the stamp's size; and a black frame no longer prints grey.
  • Saved albums, saved settings and Continue work from 6.0.0 and 5.1.0 open and print unchanged.

Release 6.0.0 — 2 November 2026

  • Grid: visible lines stay at 2.5 mm, moving and resizing snap to 1.25 mm, with faint 1.25 mm lines at 100% zoom and above only (screen only, never in Preview or PDF).
  • Arrow keys: plain arrow jumps to the next visible 2.5 mm line, Shift + arrow moves 1.25 mm, and arrow keys never snap to neighbours.
  • New spacing option Tight at 5 mm, listed in numeric order Tight 5, Narrow 10, Normal 20, Wide 30, and Auto-align never switches to Tight by itself.
  • Auto-align keeps every row on the grid: when the leftover cannot split evenly, the extra 2.5 mm goes to the right, and dividers and corner pictures are left where you put them.
  • Page borders: Small (about 0.75×), Medium (today's size), and Large (about 1.4×) sizes, with 15 new motifs and 9 new corners added (23 patterns and 17 corners in total).
  • Page borders in Dense margins: if Large does not fit outside the line, it either shrinks to the largest size that fits onto the line, or moves onto the line, and the panel states which.
  • Page border None option: default unchanged, existing albums keep their border unless None is picked, and page numbers sit just under the usable area when None is on.
  • 10 new fonts, taking the total from 8 to 18, available in every text control including captions, grouped and shown in their own typeface, with Bold and Italic greyed out where a font does not have them.
  • Snap to neighbours while dragging: edges and centres within about 1 mm pull onto a neighbour's line or the page centre, with a pink alignment line on screen only, and snapping to exactly the chosen spacing with the blue gap measurement shown.
  • Genuine overlaps go back to fix, not to print: a stamp overlapping the page border or another stamp is not printed, and you are returned to the editor to fix it; closeness alone does not block printing.
  • Saved work from 5.1.0 opens unchanged, with today's border size reading as Medium, and nothing auto-fixed on opening.
  • Corner pictures (Art tile): 23 pictures to personalise a page — 9 philatelic, 6 postal, 8 heraldic — in the page-border colour, with supporters and a scroll ribbon with motto on heraldic shields only.
  • Row dividers (Divider tile): horizontal lines, full usable width and centred by default, in 6 styles and 3 weights, moving up and down only, with an ornament option for the short centred line style.
  • Corner pictures and row dividers warn only on overlap, never block printing, and Auto-align leaves them where you put them.
