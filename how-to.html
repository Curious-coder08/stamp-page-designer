How to use Stamp Page Designer

Version 7.0.0. This guide follows the tool in the order you meet it: first screen, then the editor, then preview and saving your PDF. It is deliberately short — the tool is meant to be used, not studied. The full Guide is inside the tool (the ? Guide button).

Contents 1. The first screen 2. The editor, top to bottom 3. Stamps, text and headers 4. Clip Art, dividers, frames, Title tab and Margin text 5. Page Settings 6. Preview, Save as PDF and printing 7. Warnings, undo and shortcuts 8. Where your work lives

1. The first screen

Every time you open the tool, you land on the first screen. Nothing opens automatically.

  • Continue your last work — opens the project you were last working on, with its pages, zoom, selection, and undo history.
  • Saved albums (up to 3) — buttons showing name and date only. Clicking one loads the full album. Saving a fourth album asks which slot to replace — it never silently deletes one, and never creates a fourth.
  • Open album file… (new in 7.0) — brings back an album you saved with Download album, pictures included. You choose which of the 3 slots to keep it in; a slot that holds another album is only replaced after asking.
  • Saved settings (up to 3) — saved combinations of paper, margins, punch-hole, spacing, caption font, frame line and page border. No stamps, no pages. Applied in one step.
  • Start fresh — starts a brand new project. See §2 below for its steps.

2. The editor, top to bottom

Start fresh, step by step

  1. Save first? — “Do you want to save your current work as an album first?” Choose Save it first, Don't save, or Cancel. Cancel leaves your current project completely unchanged.
  2. If you chose Don't save — a “Discard unsaved work?” confirm appears. Discard goes forward; Cancel goes back unchanged.
  3. Name — give the new project a name.
  4. Settings — pick a saved settings combination, or Default settings, if saved settings exist.
  5. Clean page — a new page, 1 page, 0 stamps, with those settings applied. Nothing is carried over from the old project.

The view bar

  • Add — stamps (four sizes, custom, triangle), Page Header, Subheader, Description box, Text, Clip Art, Divider, Frame, Title tab and Margin text. The buttons share one row and wrap on smaller screens.
  • Auto-align — tidies the page onto the 2.5 mm grid, respecting your spacing, and never switches to Tight by itself. Every box edge lands on a visible 2.5 mm line, and stamps are never shrunk.
    • Pictures, Text boxes and fine-moved items never move; rows are laid out around them. On a page that has any, Auto-align asks first — Align around them or Cancel.
    • Dividers start a new section; headers, subheaders and dividers keep the position you chose for them.
    • A frame and everything inside it count as one item. With a frame selected, Auto-align arranges only what is inside it, and the frame grows if needed.
  • Snap to neighbours — while dragging, an item's edge or centre within about 1 mm of a neighbour's edge or centre, or the page centre, pulls onto that line, with a pink alignment line shown on screen only. It can also snap to exactly your chosen spacing from a neighbour, with the blue gap measurement shown. Keep dragging past it to break free, or hold Alt (Option on Mac) to turn neighbour snapping off for that drag.
  • Select All — selects everything on the current page. With several stamps selected, Make these the same size sets them all to one width and height in a single step (it refuses, and names them, if any would not fit).
  • Copy / Paste — copies the selected items. Paste puts them in the first free space. Copy is session-only.
  • Download album (new in 7.0) — saves the whole album, pictures included, as one .stampalbum file.
  • Zoom, Fit to page, Fit to width — zoom changes only your view, never the printed size.
  • Undo / Redo — up to 10 steps. One Undo reverts a whole applied settings combination, a whole group move, or a whole Auto-align.
  • Shortcuts — the Shortcuts button, or the ? key, shows every keyboard shortcut on one card.

3. Stamps, text and headers

Stamp boxes

  • Sizes: Small 25 × 25 mm, Medium 30 × 30 mm, Large 35 × 35 mm, Extra Large 40 × 40 mm. Custom stamps can be any rectangle, up to 210 × 210 mm.
  • Moving: drag any item from anywhere on it. A plain arrow key jumps to the next 2.5 mm line; Shift + arrow moves 1.25 mm; Alt + Shift + arrow (new) moves 0.625 mm for pocket sheets — a fine-moved item is then left alone by Auto-align. Finer grid lines appear as you zoom in (screen only).
  • Exact position (new) — a folded section at the bottom of an item's settings shows its distance from the top-left corner of the paper to its outer printed edge (left, top and bottom, in mm) — handy for measuring against a pocket sheet with a ruler. It remembers whether you left it open.
  • Resizing: stamps resize with the mouse in 2.5 mm steps, the caption moving with the box. Description boxes, Text boxes, pictures and frames resize from their blue corners.
  • Frame line (new): the stamp box is Dashed grey or Solid black — set for the whole album in Page Settings, and changeable for any single stamp.
  • Captions: a 5 mm strip below each stamp. Tick Multiple lines for up to 4 lines, each as wide as the stamp; the strip grows 5 mm per line. Caption font is set per page — Source Sans 3, 9 pt by default, from 18 fonts.
  • Text inside the frame (new): a short note inside the box (a value, a shade). It prints only if you tick Print it; otherwise it shows on screen with a crossed-out printer icon.
  • S-numbers: each stamp gets an S-number in the order it was added on that page, shown in the box's upper-left corner. S-numbers never print and are never reused.
  • Mounts: Standard mount clearance 2.5 mm per side, Strips 5 mm per side. Spacing between items: Tight 5 mm, Narrow 10 mm, Normal 20 mm, Wide 30 mm, across and down separately.
  • Triangle: one point-up triangle tile, with Base and Height inputs and a 2 mm border.

Headers and subheaders

  • Page Header: optional, one per page, heights 25 / 30 / 35 / 40 mm. Subheaders: up to 2 per page, heights 15 / 20 / 25 mm.
  • Position (new): Far left · Left · Centre · Right · Far right, once the box is narrower than full width (Narrower or Fit the width to the text). Left and Right sit 2 cm in; Far left and Far right sit against the edge of the usable area. The text follows the side, and Auto-align keeps it there.
  • Headers move up and down by dragging or with the arrow keys.

Text boxes and description boxes

  • Text (new, pale teal): a borderless label anywhere on the page, up to 4 lines — left, centre or right aligned, with line spacing (Tight, Normal, Relaxed), first line bold and first line underlined, any font, size and text colour. More than 4 lines shows red and only the first 4 print.
  • Description boxes: centred text, width 30–200 mm, height 10–200 mm, text up to 30 pt. They may sit tight against a stamp with no warning — only a real overlap is reported.

4. Clip Art, dividers, frames, Title tab and Margin text

  • Clip Art (called Art before 7.0): pressing it adds nothing yet — the panel shows everything to choose from, and a picture is added in a free spot only when you click it (or press Cancel).
    • 23 line pictures (philatelic, postal, heraldic) — in the page-border colour, any border colour, or any colour you pick.
    • 20 Classic stamps — engraved-style drawings in the style of famous early stamps (Basel Dove style, Bullseye style, Three-penny Beaver style, Bluenose schooner style …), added 40 mm on the long side.
    • 255 flags — type a few letters (“Bel”) to find one. They print in their own colours.
    • Your own picture — JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF up to 25 MB, made smaller for you automatically. iPhone HEIC photos must be saved as JPEG first. Only use pictures you are allowed to use.
  • Divider: pressing it shows the styles first — single, double, double wavy, dotted, thick-and-thin, or a short line with an ornament. Weights Thin, Medium, Thick; any colour. Narrower takes 2 cm off each side; then choose Far left · Left · Centre · Right · Far right, like headers.
  • Frame (new, pale pink): an ornamental rectangle round a stamp, a set or a row. Select stamps and press Frame (or Frame, 2.5 mm clear / 5 mm clear); the stamps keep their own borders. Lines: one, two or three; Stamp edge (perforation teeth); Stamp edge + rule; or Hidden (groups only, never printed). Thin, Medium or Thick, square or rounded corners, any colour. Everything inside moves with the frame. A frame can't go inside another frame.
  • Title tab (new): a label on the top-left or top-right corner of the page border, partly outside it, like the country tab on printed album pages. 1 or 2 lines, an optional line round it, and a flag before and/or after the text (the same flag or two different ones). The border breaks behind it and that corner's ornament is left out. You can make it wider or taller; Fit to text puts it back.
  • Margin text (new): one line reading bottom to top in the side margin — up to two on each side (Left 1, Left 2, Right 1, Right 2), each sliding up and down, with flags before and/or after. It keeps clear of the punch holes and the printer's 4 mm edge. Not with Dense margins — the edge is too crowded, so the button is greyed out there.

5. Page Settings

Page Settings is a permanent panel under the selected-item panel. It is album-wide, and its open/closed state is remembered.

  • Paper: Letter, A4, Legal, A3, plus Scott National & Specialty, Scott International, Scott Minuteman, Minkus, Davo, H. E. Harris, Stanley Gibbons, Palo, Lighthouse, White Ace, Mystic Heirloom and Schaubek — portrait only.
  • Margins: Classic or Dense. With punch-hole on, the left margin grows by ½ inch.
  • Frame line: Dashed grey or Solid black for every stamp box (each stamp can override it).
  • Page border: 26 patterns and 24 corners — new in 7.0: the Ashoka Chakra (pattern, corner and divider ornament; it turns Navy when chosen), lotus and rose patterns and corners, and seated lion, beaver, kangaroo and eagle corners, centred on the corner and upright at the bottom too. Small, Medium or Large. Border and corner colour: 8 colours or any colour (Custom…). Or None.
  • Page numbers: off by default. When on, centred under the page border, starting at 1 or any number you choose; tick Write “Page” before the number for “Page 17”.
  • Saved settings: save the current combination; applying one is one action, and one Undo reverts it.

Working colours on screen only, never printed: header pale yellow, subheader pale blue, stamps pale green, description pale grey, Clip Art pale purple, divider pale orange, Text / Title tab / Margin text pale teal, frames pale pink.

6. Preview, Save as PDF and printing

  • Preview hides the grid, working colours and warnings. What you see in Preview is the layout that prints.
  • Print / Save as PDF (new in 7.0 on the website): the tool makes the PDF itself and saves it to your Downloads — no print window. Every page is at true size and at its own paper size (one album can mix Letter, A4 and others), with sharp, searchable text. If a browser can't make it, the tool offers the browser's print window instead.
  • Then print the PDF at 100% only (Actual size), never “Fit to page”. At 100%, a 30 mm stamp box measures 30 mm — check your first print with a ruler.
  • Save album keeps the full editable project in one of the 3 slots; Download album keeps it as a file. A PDF cannot be reopened as a project.

7. Warnings, undo and shortcuts

  • Warnings appear as a folded line on the page, counted by kind of problem and by item. Opening the line lists each item with its clash partners.
  • Genuine overlaps do not print: a stamp overlapping another stamp or the page border is not printed — you are taken back to that page to fix it. Closeness alone, and pictures, dividers, text or frames overlapping something, are warned about but never block the PDF.
  • Saved work from 6.0 opens unchanged: albums, saved settings and Continue work from 6.0.0 (and 5.1.0) open exactly as saved.
  • Duplicate page creates a copy right after the original.
  • No crop marks, no landscape, no circle or pentagon shapes — by design, to keep the tool simple and the pages clean.

8. Where your work lives

Your albums live in this browser, on this device. There is no account and no cloud copy. Use Download album now and then to keep a .stampalbum file with every page and picture — Open album file… on the first screen brings it back, on this computer or another.

Safari tip: Safari may clear a website's storage if you don't visit it for about a week. Download your albums regularly.

Continue your last work is a separate, automatically kept slot, distinct from the three named albums. Your own pictures are kept once in the browser, apart from the albums.
