How to use Stamp Page Designer

Version 8.0.0. This guide follows the tool in the order you meet it: the first screen, the editor, the items you put on a page, then saving and printing. It is deliberately short — the tool is meant to be used, not studied. The full Guide is inside the tool (? Help), and the FAQ tab beside Settings answers the common questions.

1. The first screen

Every time you open the tool you land here. Nothing opens by itself.

  • Continue your last work — the album you were last working on, kept automatically.
  • Saved albums (up to 3) — name and date; click one to open it. Keeping a fourth asks which slot to replace — it never deletes one silently.
  • Open from my computer… — brings back an album you saved with Save to my computer, pictures included.
  • Restore: before import — appears after you import or reload a stamp list; brings the album back exactly as it was before.
  • Look at example pages (18)… — finished pages made with the tool. Open one to look round it; nothing you do is kept and your own work is not touched. Make a copy to edit turns it into your album.
  • Start a new album: 1. margins, 2. paper, 3. spacing, 4. album name and how many pages, 5. Look (Standard, Classic, Contemporary, Modern or Minimalist) and 6. First page (Blank, or a layout — see §8). Then Start designing.

Around the page

  • Pages (left): every page; + Add page, Duplicate page, Save as a layout…, ↑ ↓, Arrange pages…, Remove page. At the foot, ✓ Saved … ago shows your work is being kept.
  • Settings | FAQ (right): the selected item's settings — essentials first, the rest under More options — and Page settings below. The FAQ tab has short answers and a search box.
  • Above the page: Preview, Auto-align, Select All, Copy, Paste, Start fresh, Find a stamp, ⤢ More room, and zoom (−, +, Fit to width, Fit to page, 100%, 📏 Real size).
  • More room (or the F key) folds both panels and the Add bar away; « » ⌃ fold them one by one, leaving a slim strip to bring each back. Clicking an item brings Settings back by itself.
  • 📏 Real size: the first time, hold a bank card to the screen and match the blue card; from then on the page shows its true millimetres on that screen. Zoom never changes the printed size.

3. Stamps

  • Size: the four sizes, Custom (any rectangle up to 210 × 210 mm, typed in mm) or Triangle (base × height). Measure the stamp itself.
  • Caption: one line under the stamp, or tick Multiple lines for up to 4, each as wide as the stamp. Caption font is set per page (18 fonts).
  • More options: border on or off, mount (Standard 2.5 mm clearance, Strips 5 mm), frame line (Dashed, Solid or Perforated — the album default is in Page settings), and short text inside the box (a value or shade), printed only if you tick Print it.
  • S-numbers (S1, S2 …) show in the box's corner on screen only, in the order added; never printed, never reused.
  • Copy a stamp to another page: select it, Copy, go to the page, Paste — it keeps its size, frame line and caption.
  • Several stamps selected: Make these the same size sets one width and height in one step.

4. Titles and text

  • Title: one a page, 20–40 mm tall. Font, size, bold/italic/underline and colour; under More options: width (Narrower, Fit the width to the text), position (Far left … Far right), border and background.
  • Flag in the Title: None, Before, After or Both — inside the Title's box, sized to its letters, and Fit to text makes room for it.
  • Subtitle: up to two a page, 15–25 mm tall, the same text choices.
  • Text label: a borderless label anywhere, up to 4 lines; left, centre or right; line spacing; first line bold or underlined.
  • Story box: a paragraph in a box, 30–200 mm wide; it may sit tight against a stamp. A pale Fill (10% or 20%) can sit behind the text.
  • New Text labels and Story boxes start in the font you chose last for one.

5. Pictures, frames, lines, covers, Title tab and Side text

  • Picture: the panel shows everything first; a picture is added when you click one. Line pictures (philatelic, postal, heraldic) in the border colour or any colour; engraved-style classic stamps; 255 flags (type “Bel” to find Belgium); or your own picture (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF up to 25 MB — made smaller for you; save iPhone HEIC as JPEG first). Only use pictures you are allowed to use.
  • Frame: select stamps and press Frame. One, two or three lines, Stamp edge, rounded corners, any colour, 2.5 or 5 mm clear, and a pale Fill (grey, cream, pale blue, pale green or pale rose at 10% or 20%). A new frame gets the line you chose last. Everything inside moves with it.
  • Cover box (envelopes and covers): from the Envelope layouts (§8). 140 × 90 mm, 165 × 92 mm (#6¾ / C6) or your own size, at true size; one line for a plain box or two/three lines for a framed one; a pale fill if you like. A long #10 / DL envelope does not fit a portrait page.
  • Line: single, double, double wavy, dotted, thick-and-thin, or a short line with an ornament; Thin, Medium or Thick; any colour; full width or Narrower.
  • Title tab: a label on a top corner of the page border, partly outside it; 1 or 2 lines, a flag before or after; drag its corner to resize (up to 2 × 9 cm).
  • Side text (Margin ▾): one line reading up the side margin, up to two each side, with flags. Not shown with Dense margins.

Working colours on screen (never printed): Title yellow, Subtitle blue, stamps green, Story box grey, Picture purple, Line orange, Text / Title tab / Side text teal, Frame pink.

6. Moving, typing and the small toolbar

  • Drag any item. It snaps to its neighbours' edges and centres (a pink line shows); hold Alt (Option on a Mac) to place it freely — a reminder shows while you drag.
  • Arrow keys: plain = next 2.5 mm line; Shift = 1.25 mm; Alt+Shift = 0.625 mm for pocket sheets.
  • Exact position (folded, at the bottom of an item's settings): the distance from the paper's top-left corner to the outer printed edge, in mm.
  • Type on the page: double-click a Title, Subtitle, Text label, Story box, Title tab or Side text — or a stamp, for its caption. Enter keeps it, Shift+Enter starts a new line where allowed, Escape cancels.
  • The small toolbar above the selected item: for a stamp S M L XL, ✎ Caption, Copy, Frame, Delete; for text ✎ Edit text, Copy, Delete.
  • Auto-align evens out the spaces onto the 2.5 mm grid, never shrinking stamps; pictures, Text labels and fine-moved items stay put (it asks first). With a frame selected, it arranges only what is inside.

7. Page settings: look, mounts, border

  • Album look — every page: Standard (the version 7 look), Classic (serif, boxed title, ornament corners), Contemporary (clean sans, soft title band), Modern (bold, title on the left), Minimalist (light sans, no border). Changing it asks first, moves nothing, and one Undo brings the old look back.
  • My mounts add — 0 mm (hinges) to 3 mm, the whole album. Keep typing the stamp's own size; the border is drawn round the stamp plus its mount (a 30 mm stamp with 2 mm mounts prints a 39 mm border). If a stamp then reaches the border, use Fix: Auto-align this page.
  • Paper (Letter, A4, Legal, A3 and 12 album brands), margins (Classic or Dense, with punch-hole), spacing (Tight 5, Narrow 10, Normal 20, Wide 30 mm), frame line, page border (26 patterns, 24 corners, Small/Medium/Large, or None) and page numbers.
  • Saved settings (up to 3): paper, margins, spacing, caption font, frame line and border in one step.

8. Pages and page layouts

  • + Add page opens the layouts. At the top choose the stamp size (or your own) and the cover size; the pictures redraw.
    • Grids: Single feature (one stamp and its story), Pair, 2 × 2, 3 × 3, 4 × 4.
    • Rows: Country page, 2 + 3 + 2, 2 + 3 + 3, 3 + 2 + 3, 3 + 3, 1 + 2 + 3, 1 large + 4, Definitive set, Set of four.
    • Special: Envelope: cover, Envelope: cover story, Souvenir sheet + singles, Strips, Triangle row.
    • Your layouts — pages you saved with Save as a layout… (up to 12, in every album).
    Stamps are never shrunk: a layout that doesn't fit says so (“Doesn't fit at Extra large — try Large”). A card that says “Uses Narrow spacing” or “Uses Dense margins” fits only that way. Every layout follows the album's paper, margins, border and look.
  • Duplicate page copies the page right after it — the quickest way to the next page of a series.
  • Arrange pages… shows every page as a picture: drag them into order (or ← →), then Done. One Undo puts them back.
  • Remove page asks first; Undo brings it back.

9. Save PDF and printing

  1. Preview hides the grid, working colours and warnings — it is what prints.
  2. Press Save PDF. Choose All pages, This page or Pages … to …; a picture of the first page is shown. Press Make the PDF.
  3. Chrome and Edge ask where to save it; Safari and Firefox put it in Downloads. The PDF is made in the tool: true size, each page at its own paper size, sharp text.
  4. Print at 100% only (Actual size) — never “Fit to page”. A 30 mm box then measures 30 mm: check your first print with a ruler.

A stamp overlapping another stamp or the page border stops the PDF and takes you to it; closeness and other overlaps are only warned about. A PDF can't be opened back in the tool — keep the album too (§10).

10. Saving your album

  • Your work is kept in this browser automatically as you go (✓ Saved … ago) — that is Continue your last work.
  • Save album ▾ → Keep in this browser… keeps it in one of 3 named slots, in this browser only.
  • Save album ▾ → Save to my computer saves one .stampalbum file — every page, the setup and your pictures. Copy or email it; Open from my computer… brings it back on any computer. Do this now and then: there is no account and no cloud copy.
  • Safari may clear a website's storage if you don't visit for about a week — save to your computer regularly.

11. Warnings, Undo and shortcuts

  • Warnings appear above the page, grouped and counted. Under them, Fix: Auto-align this page evens out the spaces and Show me selects the items concerned.
  • Undo / Redo: up to 10 steps; point at Undo to see the step (“Undo: Add stamp S4”). Ctrl+Z / Cmd+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. While you type in a box, these undo your typing instead.
  • ⌨ (or the ? key) shows every shortcut; F = More room; Delete removes the selection; Escape clears it.

12. Find a stamp

The box above the page (“Catalogue no. or caption”) searches the whole album. Type part of a catalogue number or caption, or a key such as 3_S2 (page 3, stamp S2), and press Enter. One match: it goes to the page, selects the stamp and makes it flash. Several: a short list to choose from.

13. Spreadsheet & inventory

Under Page settings, a folded Spreadsheet & inventory section. You never need it for ordinary pages.

Import a stamp list

  1. Press ⬇ Get the example file. It opens in Excel or Numbers with every column and sample rows.
  2. Replace the sample rows with your stamps and save as CSV (XML works too).
  3. Press Import stamp list… — or, on an empty page, simply drop the file onto the page.
  4. If the album already has pages, choose This album (new pages after yours) or A new album (same setup). A restore point is kept first either way.

  • A page given too many stamps: the main choice is Put the extra stamps on a new page; Tight spacing and Dense margins are offered too.
  • Undo takes the import back in one step; Restore: before import on the first screen brings back the whole album as it was.

Download and reload the stamp list

Download stamp list writes every stamp with a Key (1_S1 = page 1, stamp S1). Change it in your spreadsheet — not the Key column — then Reload stamp list…. You see a summary first (updated · new · missing); missing stamps are removed only if you say so. It is one Undo step.

Inventory

Inventory… lists every stamp — page, key, catalogue number, caption, size, owned or wanted, frame, section — with counts on top. Click a heading to sort, a row to go to the stamp. Download inventory saves it with totals for Excel or Numbers. Never printed.
